## Description: <br>
Provides lightweight trademark pre-checks, similarity scoring, Vienna Classification support, Nice Classification guidance, and trademark application workflow navigation for small businesses and individual entrepreneurs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, small businesses, individual entrepreneurs, and developers use this skill to perform preliminary trademark name or graphical mark checks, estimate similarity risk, identify likely Nice classes, and understand trademark application, renewal, change, transfer, and non-use cancellation workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Trademark search results and workflow guidance may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for legal filing decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary research only, verify current records through official trademark databases, and consult a qualified trademark professional for filing decisions or disputes. <br>
Risk: Similarity scoring and classification suggestions may miss pending applications, blind-period records, or jurisdiction-specific requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Repeat searches close to filing, review official records for the target jurisdiction, and use professional review before submitting or responding to trademark matters. <br>
