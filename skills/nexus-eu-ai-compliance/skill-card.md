## Description: <br>
Automate EU AI Act compliance for organizations by classifying AI systems by risk level, generating Article 11 documentation, implementing bias testing in CI/CD, and preparing conformity assessments. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[shuwanito](https://clawhub.ai/user/shuwanito) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Legal, governance, and engineering teams can use this skill to inventory AI systems, classify EU AI Act risk levels, draft compliance documentation, plan remediation, and prepare audit-ready conformity assessment materials. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated classifications, documentation, or remediation steps may be incomplete or incorrect if treated as legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified legal or compliance reviewers validate outputs against current EU AI Act, GDPR, and national requirements before use. <br>
Risk: The skill may read organizational project files and AI-system inventory data when directed. <br>
Mitigation: Run it only in workspaces approved for processing compliance documentation and AI inventory information. <br>
