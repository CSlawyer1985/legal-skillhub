# 离婚诉讼文书专家 / PRC Divorce Litigation Document Expert

## 中文简介

离婚诉讼文书专家（离婚文书）是一款面向婚姻家事律师、法务及当事人的法律文书技能，擅长离婚纠纷**民事起诉状**与**民事答辩状**的要素式起草、审查与修改，并内置完整的婚姻法规范依据库。

**核心能力**
- 离婚民事起诉状起草与修改（8 项诉讼请求：解除婚姻、夫妻共同财产、共同债务、子女抚养、抚养费、探望权、离婚损害赔偿/经济补偿/经济帮助、诉讼费用）
- 离婚民事答辩状起草与修改（对原告诉请逐项确认/异议）
- 法律论证与条文精确援引（民法典婚姻家庭编第1040–1118条 + 最高人民法院解释（一）（法释〔2020〕22号）、解释（二）（法释〔2025〕1号））
- 事实与理由撰写及证据清单整理

**内置法规库（精确援引条号）**
- 《民法典》第五编 婚姻家庭 全文（第1040–1118条）
- 婚姻家庭编解释（一）全文（91条）
- 婚姻家庭编解释（二）全文（23条，2025-02-01施行，含房产加名、父母出资购房、直播打赏挥霍、股权分割、抢夺藏匿未成年子女等新规）
- 民事诉讼法离婚诉讼程序要点（管辖/调解/保全/送达/缺席判决）
- 最高人民法院要素式起诉状/答辩状模板（含填写实例要点）

**来源经验**：依法处理众多离婚诉讼案件，处理过众多离婚诉讼相关法律文书。

## English Description

The PRC Divorce Litigation Document Expert ("Divorce Counsel") is a legal-document skill for family-law practitioners and clients. It drafts and reviews **element-style civil complaints** and **civil answers** for divorce disputes under PRC law, bundled with a complete marriage-family statute library.

**Core capabilities**
- Drafting & revising divorce civil complaints (8 claim categories: dissolution, marital property, marital debt, child custody, child support, visitation, divorce damages/economic compensation/economic assistance, litigation costs)
- Drafting & revising divorce civil answers (confirm/object to each plaintiff claim)
- Legal reasoning with precise citations (Civil Code Marriage-and-Family Book arts.1040–1118 + SPC Interpretation (I) (FaShi〔2020〕No.22) and Interpretation (II) (FaShi〔2025〕No.1))
- Fact-and-reason narrative and evidence-list organization

**Bundled statutes (cite to article/paragraph/item)**
- Civil Code, Book V Marriage and Family (arts.1040–1118)
- SPC Interpretation (I) on Marriage and Family (91 articles)
- SPC Interpretation (II) on Marriage and Family (23 articles, effective 2025-02-01: property name-addition, parent-funded home purchase, livestream tipping as dissipation, equity division, child abduction, etc.)
- Key Civil Procedure Law points for divorce litigation (jurisdiction/mediation/preservation/service/default judgment)
- Supreme People's Court element-style complaint/answer templates (with example highlights)

**Experience base**: handled numerous divorce litigation cases and their legal documents.
