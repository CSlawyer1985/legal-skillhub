## [1.0.0] - 2026-03-13

### Added
- Initial release of tax compliance and planning skill
- Comprehensive VAT documentation including 2026 VAT Law implementation
- Corporate Income Tax, Personal Income Tax, and other major tax types
- Six core capabilities:
  - Tax Policy Identification and Analysis
  - Tax Compliance Assessment and Reporting
  - Tax Planning and Optimization
  - Tax Risk Assessment and Management
  - Tax Audit Support
  - Transfer Pricing Analysis (for multi-jurisdiction businesses)
- Detailed reference documentation:
  - `tax_types_china.md`: Overview of major Chinese tax types
  - `vat_special_topic.md`: In-depth VAT analysis with 2026 new regulations
  - `compliance_report_template.md`: Complete compliance report template
  - `tax_planning_checklist.md`: Comprehensive planning checklist
  - `tax_audit_preparation_guide.md`: Detailed audit preparation guide
  - `api_reference.md`: API documentation for tax-related services

### Key Features
- VAT taxpayer identity selection strategies with tax burden balance point calculations
- Input tax credit management and optimization guidelines
- Preferential VAT policies (immediate refund, additional deduction, export refund)
- Tax rate differential utilization and business segmentation strategies
- Risk management and compliance requirements
- Common Q&A and practical case studies
- Integration with official tax authority documents and policies

### Documentation
- Based on authoritative sources:
  - 《中华人民共和国增值税法》
  - 《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》
  - Ministry of Finance and State Administration of Taxation announcements
  - Big Four accounting firm interpretations
  - 96 official implementation Q&A
  - 10+ supporting policy announcements

### Important Notes
- Loan service input tax credit remains non-deductible (Article 21 of Implementation Regulations)
- Simplified taxation with 3% levy rate (some 5% rate projects extended to end of 2027)
- Long-term asset mixed-use input tax credit rules (full deduction for assets under 5 million yuan, gradual adjustment for larger assets)
