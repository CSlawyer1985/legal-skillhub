## Description: <br>
Legal Documents Cellcog helps agents draft legal documents, contracts, policies, compliance reviews, and legal research outputs through CellCog from natural language prompts, with outputs explicitly not treated as legal advice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, employees, and developers use this skill to ask CellCog to draft contracts, NDAs, terms of service, privacy policies, legal memos, compliance reviews, and related legal research outputs. Important legal documents should be reviewed by a qualified attorney before execution, especially for high-value, regulated, or jurisdiction-specific matters. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Legal documents generated by AI may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for a specific jurisdiction or transaction. <br>
Mitigation: Have a qualified attorney review important documents before execution, especially for high-value, regulated, or jurisdiction-specific matters. <br>
Risk: Sensitive legal or business information may be sent to the CellCog service when prompts are submitted. <br>
Mitigation: Use CELLCOG_API_KEY only in trusted environments and avoid submitting highly sensitive information unless CellCog's privacy, retention, and confidentiality terms meet your requirements. <br>
Risk: The skill depends on the third-party CellCog package and service. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that you trust the CellCog package and service before installation or use. <br>
