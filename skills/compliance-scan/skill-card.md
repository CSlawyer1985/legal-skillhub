## Description: <br>
Scans code repositories or data-processing flows for GDPR, CCPA, SOC 2, HIPAA, and PCI-DSS risks, then produces structured findings and remediation guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, engineering teams, compliance officers, and CTOs use this skill to self-check codebases or data workflows for privacy, security, and industry compliance issues before audit preparation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Compliance reports may expose sensitive code, secrets, or regulated personal data from the scanned material. <br>
Mitigation: Limit scan scope to necessary files, avoid unnecessary regulated datasets, and require redaction of secrets and sensitive personal data in the report. <br>
Risk: Automated findings may be incomplete or unsuitable as a final legal or audit determination. <br>
Mitigation: Use the report as an internal self-check and have qualified compliance or legal reviewers confirm conclusions before relying on them for formal audits. <br>
