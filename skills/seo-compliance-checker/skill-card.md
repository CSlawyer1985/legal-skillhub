## Description: <br>
SEO compliance checker with real-time API backend for Chinese content, including advertising-law banned word scanning, SEO compliance checks, and safer replacement suggestions for Baidu, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Taobao, and JD content. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Content creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to check or generate Chinese platform content for advertising-law wording risks, banned-word replacements, keyword density, title optimization, and platform-specific SEO guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can send draft Chinese marketing content, product claims, titles, and keywords to a remote API without clear privacy or retention terms in the evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for content that is acceptable to share with the listed remote API, and avoid confidential customer data, unreleased campaigns, regulated business information, or sensitive internal copy unless the publisher provides acceptable privacy and retention terms. <br>
