# Brand and trademark boundary

Apache-2.0 licenses copyright and patent rights as stated in `LICENSE`; it
does not grant a general license to use the CSlawyer name, logo, or other
brand identifiers.

Downstream users may identify a fork or integration as based on
`legal-meta-skill` when that statement is accurate. They must not call a fork
the official CSlawyer release, use CSlawyer branding as an endorsement, or
remove required copyright and NOTICE information. A separate written brand
permission is required for official-looking co-branding.
