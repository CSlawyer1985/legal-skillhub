## Description: <br>
Generate a prioritised compliance checklist for GDPR, SOC 2, ISO 27001, FCA, HIPAA, or other frameworks with gap analysis, evidence requirements, quick wins, and audit-readiness priorities. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, compliance teams, and operators use this skill to prepare tailored readiness checklists and gap analyses for regulatory or audit frameworks. The skill is for planning support and explicitly requires professional legal or compliance review before relying on its outputs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Compliance checklist outputs may be incomplete, outdated, or inappropriate for a specific jurisdiction or audit context. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the skill's disclaimer visible and require qualified legal or compliance professionals to review outputs before regulatory claims, audit submissions, or programme implementation. <br>
Risk: Users may disclose unnecessary sensitive internal compliance details while requesting a checklist. <br>
Mitigation: Ask only for the framework, organisation profile, maturity, deadline, and high-level scope needed to tailor the checklist; avoid collecting credentials or unnecessary confidential records. <br>
