## Description: <br>
Global compliance checker with API-powered regulations database for GDPR readiness, CCPA compliance, data localization, cross-border data transfer, payment licensing, content moderation laws, AI Act requirements, China outbound data transfer rules, compliance gap analysis, remediation roadmaps, CLI regulation lookup, and App Store review checklists. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External developers, product teams, and compliance reviewers use this skill to assess overseas launch requirements for Chinese apps, SaaS products, e-commerce services, and content platforms. It helps identify applicable regulations, compliance gaps, cross-border transfer needs, app store readiness issues, and remediation priorities. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may direct users to Tencent-hosted API and web services, creating confidentiality and privacy exposure for submitted product, customer, legal, or marketing data. <br>
Mitigation: Review the external service privacy and retention terms before use, and avoid sending confidential product plans, customer data, legal documents, or unpublished marketing copy unless approved. <br>
Risk: Compliance guidance can be incomplete or out of date because regulations and platform review policies change frequently. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as preliminary analysis, verify current requirements for each target market, and consult qualified legal counsel before launch decisions. <br>
